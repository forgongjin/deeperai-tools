Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Our Core Promise

DeeperAI Tools is built on a privacy-first philosophy. Every tool we offer — image compression, PDF merging, splitting, watermarking — runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device, not even for a second.

What We Collect

The short answer: almost nothing.

  • No file uploads: All processing happens locally using browser APIs (Canvas, Web Workers, pdf-lib). We never receive, store, or see your files.
  • No registration required: Use any tool without creating an account.
  • Anonymous analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page views and traffic sources. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Advertising: This site uses Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve personalized ads. See Google's Advertising Policies.

Cookies

We use cookies through Google Analytics and Google AdSense for traffic analysis and ad delivery. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. We do not use our own tracking cookies.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4: Anonymized traffic analytics
  • Google AdSense: Ad delivery
  • GitHub Pages: Static site hosting (no backend)
  • Lemonsqueezy: Payment processing (only when you upgrade to Pro)

Data Security

Since everything runs in your browser, there's no transmission risk. Even if our site were compromised, attackers couldn't access your files — because we never had them to begin with.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
