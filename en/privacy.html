Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

🔒 Key Principle: All file processing happens entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server. We cannot see, access, or store your files.

We collect minimal information. Specifically:

Any information we collect is used solely to:

4. Data Security

Since all file processing happens locally in your browser, there is no risk of your files being intercepted during upload or storage on our servers — because they are never transmitted in the first place. Our website is served over HTTPS, ensuring that the web page itself is delivered securely.
