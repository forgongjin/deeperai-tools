About Deeper AI Tools

We build free, privacy-first online tools that help you get work done — without compromising your data.

Our Mission

Most online tools require you to upload your files to a server — which means your data leaves your device. We believe that shouldn't be necessary. All of our tools process files directly in your browser using client-side technology. Your images, your PDFs, your data — they stay on your device, period.

What We Offer

  • Image Compressor — Batch compress JPG, PNG, WebP images with quality control and format conversion.
  • PDF Tools — Merge, split, extract pages, compress, and add watermarks to PDF files.

More tools are on the way — all following the same principle: local processing, zero uploads.

Why "Deeper AI"?

We believe the best tools work quietly in the background, handling the complex stuff so you can focus on what matters. Whether it's compressing product images for your online store or merging PDF reports for a client presentation — our tools handle the technical heavy lifting while you stay in control.

Our Commitment

  • Privacy First: No file uploads. No server-side processing. No data collection.
  • Free Core Features: Essential tools are free to use with generous limits.
  • No Registration: Use our tools immediately — no account, no email, no signup.
  • Continuous Improvement: We regularly update our tools based on user feedback and evolving web standards.
